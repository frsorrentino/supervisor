- **0.8.48 — 2026-10-23: the prompt's origin is resolved after the hook, not during it (regression of 0.8.46).**
  - Reported by the master at 16:22: since 11:55 no inbox had a `terminal` record; every prompt the maintainer typed was `unverified` (master 5, supervisor 3, another session 16), so an ok typed in a terminal could not be registered. Cause: Claude Code 2.1.308 and 2.1.309 append the prompt's `user` line to the transcript after the hooks have run (physically after the turn's `api-request` line; `history.jsonl` too), so at hook time the line never exists; 0.8.46 waited a second for it, and its tests passed on a fake transcript written before the hook. Two prompts of the master (13:24, 13:35) were lost to the 5 s timeout of sessions started before 0.8.46 (`hook_cancelled`, 8-9 s under load 8-11).
  - Now the hook records the prompt at once as `unverified`, with the transcript's path, and no longer waits. `cm-inbox.py` `resolve` reads the line later (`find_prompt_line`: same text, within three minutes; tails of 256 KB, 4 MB, 64 MB) and rewrites the record: `human` → `terminal`, `scheduled` → `cron` (from `cron`), `peer` and `task_notification` → the record goes. Resolution runs at the session's next prompt (for the records before it), at its Stop, in `supervisor inbox` and inside `task approve --inbox` at the moment of use: an ok typed a few seconds earlier is good; one the transcript never shows is refused with «try again in a few seconds».
  - Tests: H8 rewritten (the transcript line arrives after the hook), T21b (registry), IB12 (inbox).
